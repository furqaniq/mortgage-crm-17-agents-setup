Mortgage CRM · Agent Roster

Seventeen agents,
one front door.

17 agents · 4 divisions · 14 hireable roles
Runtime: Paperclip · Apps: Composio
Handbook v6 · Rev. 2026-09-17

This is the final roster for solo loan officers and small teams. Every agent has two names: a codename used in prompts and logs, and a job title for the one job it does. The storefront hire a client buys can combine several agents.

People reach the company only through SOPHIA, in the CRM's chat. SOPHIA answers questions about CRM data directly, without changing anything, and passes everything that needs doing to ATLAS, the only agent that assigns work. AEGIS checks everything that leaves the company and answers to the Account Owner, not to either of them.

Command

Every request passes through these three. All three are included in every plan.

01SOPHIA

Personal Assistant

The chat front door for everyone on the team. It answers questions from the CRM and each person's own connected apps without changing anything, passes every instruction that needs work to ATLAS, and delivers results, briefs, escalations and AEGIS findings.

Hires as
Personal Assistant
Reports to
Account Owner (Paperclip CEO, reporting to the board)
Autonomy
L3
Included for the Account Owner; priced per additional seat
Owns
The CRM chat bot, per-user threads and memory, working-style profiles
Hard boundary

Carries messages; never decides them. It cannot approve anything, cannot advance, reject or remove an AEGIS review stage, and never softens, delays or withholds an AEGIS finding.

Measured on — instructions reaching ATLAS with intent and scope right the first time · instructions sent anywhere other than ATLAS (target zero) · direct CRM answers that were accurate and within the user's permissions · CRM writes by SOPHIA (target zero) · answers drawn from the wrong user's connected accounts (target zero) · decisions surfaced versus missed · time to first action each morning · commands executed without menu navigation · AEGIS findings delivered unaltered (must be 100%) · reported time saved

02ATLAS

Chief of Staff

Turns every instruction and inbound signal into a multi-agent plan, delegates each piece to the right specialist, keeps the per-contact memory brief, and returns one result to SOPHIA. It is the only agent that assigns work, and it does no specialist work itself.

Hires as
Chief of Staff
Reports to
SOPHIA
Autonomy
L4
Included in every plan
Owns
Work plans and every agent task in Paperclip; signal routing; the per-contact memory brief; per-contact, per-campaign, per-user and per-branch budget halts
Hard boundary

No authority over AEGIS, despite sitting above it in the org tree. It can't change AEGIS's instructions, role or budget, and can't advance, reject or remove its review stage.

Measured on — routing accuracy · tasks completed unattended · escalation precision · cost per completed task · outbound tasks created without an AEGIS review stage (target zero)

instruction files/Command/ATLAS/AGENTS.md

03AEGIS

Compliance Officer

Checks all outbound communication: rules enforced in the gated send tools and the generation screen, and a judgment review on every outbound task. It approves content before it can be sent, rules on every compliance finding, scores conversations, and keeps the compliance audit trail.

Hires as
Compliance Officer
Answers to
Account Owner, never ATLAS or SOPHIA (org tree: under ATLAS, for escalation routing only)
Autonomy
L3 cannot be disabled
Included in every plan
Owns
The rules inside every gated tool and the voice platform; the suppression list; the disclosure builder; approval IDs; review stages; the compliance audit trail
Hard boundary

Answers to the Account Owner, not to ATLAS or SOPHIA. Its configuration and budget change only by board act, and no agent, including a chat request made through SOPHIA, can override its blocks.

Measured on — unconsented sends (target zero) · gate latency · QA coverage · outbound tasks closed without AEGIS review (target zero) · connected-app sends without a matching gate pass (target zero) · sends of unapproved content outside real-time channels (target zero) · audit export time · red-team findings closed

12CANVAS

Graphic Designer

Owns what the company looks like. It makes ad visuals, social graphics, carousels, decks and co-branded visuals, keeps the brand record's visual identity, and runs the content library with every image's source and rights recorded. Every image draft passes the generation screen, and nothing is used until AEGIS approves it.

Hires as
Part of Marketing Coordinator / Partner Manager
Reports to
ATLAS
Autonomy
L2
Owns
Brand Guideline (visual identity), Content Library
Hard boundary

Never generates, edits or uses the likeness of a real, identifiable person, except a staff member's own profile photo used with their recorded consent, or a partner's own headshot supplied with their written consent. Never uses an image whose source or rights are unknown, and imagery never implies a preferred demographic.

Measured on — brand consistency across published assets · asset reuse rate · production volume · library search success · assets in use with unknown source or rights (target zero)

Digital Campaign Specialist

It owns audiences, deliverability, SMS carrier registration, per-contact timing and statistically sound tests, and paces bulk sends so a campaign slows down rather than failing half-sent. Posts and ads go out only through gated publish tools, and comments on them go to ECHO.

Hires as
Part of Marketing Coordinator
Reports to
ATLAS
Autonomy
L2
Owns
Email, SMS, Social Planner, Ad Manager, Custom Campaigns
Hard boundary

Never spends more on a paid campaign than a board user approved, never changes an ad account's billing, payment method or spend limit, and never uploads contact data from the CRM to an ad platform. A paid audience never selects by a protected class or a proxy for one.

Measured on — inbox placement · delivery rate · complaint and bounce rates · test velocity · cost per engaged contact · cost per lead from paid social · spend over the approved amount (target zero) · live posts or ads carrying a revoked approval (target zero)
